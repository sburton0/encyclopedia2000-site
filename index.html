Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, purchases, subscriptions, or accounts. If you'd like to help, you can donate to Wikipedia or tip the developer.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Articles and pictures come live from Wikipedia. Offline reading is planned.

Does it collect any information about me?

No. Your favorites and settings stay on your computer. Read the privacy policy.

Why does Windows warn me when I run it?

Windows SmartScreen is cautious about new apps. Encyclopedia 2000 is signed by its publisher; if you see the warning, click More info → Run anyway.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Use Help → Send Feedback in the app, join the Discord, or use the feedback form.
