FAQ

Is it made by Wikipedia?

No. Encyclopedia 2000 is an independent project by one developer. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Wikipedia or the Wikimedia Foundation; it shows Wikipedia's freely licensed articles and credits them. To support Wikipedia itself, donate to the Wikimedia Foundation.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, purchases, subscriptions, or accounts. If you'd like to help, you can donate to Wikipedia or tip the developer.

What does "Beta" mean?

Encyclopedia 2000 is still being built. New features arrive often, and you may run into the occasional rough edge. On Windows and Mac, updates download and install by themselves. If something isn't working, use Help → Send Feedback in the app.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not if you download an offline library. Normally, articles and pictures come live from Wikipedia. In Tools → Offline Library you can download Wikipedia to your computer (the best 220,000 articles in 7 GB, or all of it) and read, search, and explore without the internet. Videos, sounds, and a few live features still need a connection.

Does it collect any information about me?

No. Your favorites and settings stay on your computer. Read the privacy policy.

Why does Windows warn me when I run it?

Windows SmartScreen warns about apps it hasn't seen many people download yet, which happens with each new version for its first days. Encyclopedia 2000 is signed by its publisher. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway; your browser may also ask you to keep the download. The warning goes away as more people download the new version.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Use Help → Send Feedback in the app, join the Discord, or use the feedback form.
